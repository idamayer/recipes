# Chickpeas & Sausage in Tomato Sauce

> A hearty pantry meal that improves by the next day.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup dried chickpeas
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 lb ground pork sausage
- 1 large onion
- ½–1 red bell pepper
- ¼ tube tomato paste (about 1¼ oz / 35 g)
- 1 cup broth *(optional)*
- *Optional:* a few potatoes
- *Optional:* eggs

---

## Method

### 01 — Cook the Chickpeas

Pressure cook:
- **Soaked:** High Pressure 15–20 minutes, natural release.
- **Unsoaked:** High Pressure 35–40 minutes, natural release.

### 02 — Brown the Meat

Mince the onion. Fry the onion and meat together until browned, making sure to break up the sausage. Remove the meat mixture.

### 03 — Make the Sauce

In the same pan, fry the bell pepper and tomato paste in the residual oil.

Add the crushed tomatoes and broth, if using. Cook down for 20–30 minutes.

### 04 — Optional Additions


If using potatoes, cube them, boil in the broth, and mix in.

If using eggs, transfer the sauce to a shallow pan, make small wells, crack in the eggs, cover, and cook over medium-low heat (or in the oven) until the whites are set and the yolks reach your preferred doneness.

### 05 - Finish

Stir in the chickpeas and sausage mixture.
