# Broccoli Cheddar Soup

A hearty, rustic soup with tender broccoli, soft potatoes, caramelized onion, sharp cheddar, and a savory, tangy broth enriched with Greek yogurt and nutritional yeast.

## Ingredients

- 1 large onion, diced
- Oil, for sautéing, plus a little extra for the roux
- 2 generous spoonfuls all-purpose flour
- ¼–½ cup red cooking wine
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar *(optional, if using fresh wine instead of older cooking wine)*
- 2 × 10 oz (283 g) packages frozen chopped broccoli
- 2 red potatoes, very finely minced
- 1½–2 cups milk
- 1–1½ cups Greek yogurt
- Approximately 2 oz strong cheddar cheese, grated
- A generous amount of nutritional yeast
- Salt, to taste
- Western herb seasoning mix, to taste
- A dash of liquid smoke *(optional)*

---

## To Serve

- Sliced green onions
- Cooked, crumbled bacon *(optional)*

---

## Method

### 01 — Caramelize the Onion

Dice the onion and cook it in oil over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until deeply softened, golden, and caramelized. Allow time for the onion to develop sweetness and flavor.

### 02 — Make the Roux

Add a little more oil to the onions, followed by 2 generous spoonfuls of flour. Stir until the flour is incorporated and coats the onions. Cook briefly, stirring, to remove the raw flour taste.

### 03 — Deglaze with Red Wine

Pour in ¼–½ cup red cooking wine, scraping the bottom of the pot to release the browned bits. If using fresh wine rather than an older cooking wine, add approximately 2 tablespoons of red wine vinegar, adjusting to taste.

### 04 — Add the Vegetables and Milk

Add both packages of frozen chopped broccoli and the two very finely minced red potatoes. Pour in 1½–2 cups milk and stir well. Simmer gently until the potatoes are tender and the broccoli is cooked through, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.

### 05 — Enrich and Season

Stir in 1–1½ cups Greek yogurt, approximately 2 oz grated strong cheddar, and a generous amount of nutritional yeast. Season with salt and Western herb seasoning mix to taste. Add a dash of liquid smoke if desired. Keep the heat low after adding the yogurt and cheese, avoiding a vigorous boil.

### 06 — Finish and Serve

Leave the soup chunky; do not blend. Simmer gently until the flavors have come together, adding a splash of milk if needed to adjust the consistency. Taste and adjust the salt, herbs, and acidity. Serve hot, topped with sliced green onions and, if desired, crumbled cooked bacon.

---

## A Note from the Kitchen

This soup relies on caramelized onions, strong cheddar, and nutritional yeast for a deep, savory flavor. The Greek yogurt adds tang and creaminess, while finely minced potatoes soften into the broth and help thicken it without blending. A dash of liquid smoke adds a subtle smoky depth.
