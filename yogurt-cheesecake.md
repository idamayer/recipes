> A low sugar "breakfast cheesecake".

## Ingredients

- 1 premade graham cracker crust
- 16 oz brick-style cream cheese
- 1½ - 2 cups thick Greek yogurt, preferably full-fat
- 2 whole eggs **or** 4 egg whites
- 2-4 Tbsp flour
- Pinch of salt *(optional)*

## Method

### 01 — Soften the cream cheese

Preheat the oven to **350°F**. Remove the cream cheese from its packaging and place it in a microwave-safe bowl. Break it up with a fork and heat it **~30 seconds**, until it is very soft and easy to beat. Do not let it get hot or melted. Beat the softened cream cheese until completely smooth and creamy.

### 02 — Mix the filling

Mix in the Greek yogurt, eggs and salt until just combined.

### 03 — Fill and bake

Set the  crust on a baking sheet to catch spills. Pour the filling into the premade graham cracker crust and smooth the
top. Bake at **350°F for about 60--75 minutes**.

The cheesecake is done when the edges are clearly set and the center still has a small, slow wobble when the pan is gently nudged. It
should not look liquid in the middle. If the top is browning too quickly, tent it with foil and continue baking.

### 04 — Cool before cutting

Remove the cheesecake and let it cool completely at room temperature.

Ideally, refrigerate for **at least 4 hours**, before slicing, otherwise it will crumble. The texture becomes substantially firmer and creamier as it chills.
